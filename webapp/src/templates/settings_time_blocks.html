Sleep & Leisure Time

Weekly recurring hours -- no dates, just a time-of-day range and which days it applies to (e.g. 00:00–05:59 Monday-Sunday). The Week and Day calendar views hatch these hours (red for Sleep, green for Leisure) and warn -- but never block -- when an event or scheduled work session is placed inside one.
