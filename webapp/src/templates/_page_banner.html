{# Any banner with a `version` (uploaded, or a legacy searched banner that was downloaded and stored locally while the web-search feature existed) is served via /banners/image (routers/banners.py) instead of being embedded inline as a base64 data URI -- embedding a multi-MB blob directly in the HTML was what made pages with a banner load at 100ms+/2MB+ while identical pages without one were ~7ms. The separate request keeps the page small and cacheable, and the ?v= (a content hash) makes the immutable-cached URL change when the image does. Eager + high priority makes the image travel with the page instead.
