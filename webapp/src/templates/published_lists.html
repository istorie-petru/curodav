{{ icon('share-2') }} Published lists

A Published List is a named boolean filter over labels, for one entity type (task/event/contact). The app keeps a real Radicale collection in sync with whatever currently matches the filter and gives you a URL any standard CalDAV/CardDAV client can subscribe to. Read-only in this version -- a subscriber can view the list, but editing through the subscribed URL will be silently overwritten on the next sync, not merged back.
